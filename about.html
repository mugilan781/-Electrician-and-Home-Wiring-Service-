Our Story

Built on Trust,
Powered by Expertise

ElectraPro was founded in 2009 by master electrician James Hartwell with a simple philosophy: homeowners deserve the same level of electrical quality found in premium commercial projects — delivered with transparency, speed, and genuine care.

What started as a two-person operation has grown into a 35-strong team of licensed master and journeyman electricians, serving thousands of homes across the metro area. Every technician shares James's commitment to technical excellence and customer-first service.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

The principles that guide every service call, every wire, every connection we make.

Safety First

We never compromise on safety. Every job follows strict NEC codes, OSHA guidelines, and our own rigorous internal safety protocols.

Precision Excellence

We approach every project — from a simple outlet to a full rewire — with the same meticulous attention to detail and engineering precision.

Honest Transparency

Upfront pricing. Clear communication. No hidden fees. We believe in complete transparency — before, during, and after every job.

Rapid Response

When you need us, we're there. Our dispatching system ensures the nearest available tech reaches you as fast as possible.

Continuous Growth

Our team participates in ongoing training to stay current with the latest electrical technologies, codes, and best practices.

Community Care

We're invested in the communities we serve. We offer free electrical safety inspections to seniors and donate 2% of profits to local causes.
