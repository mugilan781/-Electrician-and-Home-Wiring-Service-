Questions

Booking FAQs

How quickly will you respond to my booking request?
We confirm all online booking requests within 1 hour during business hours. For emergency requests, we dispatch immediately and call you back within 10 minutes.
Do I need to be home for the assessment?
Yes. A responsible adult must be present for both the initial assessment and the actual work. This ensures we can properly assess the electrical system and that all decisions are made with your knowledge and approval.
Can I get a quote online without a home visit?
For standard services like outlet installation, fan installation, and simple repairs, we can often provide a ballpark estimate over the phone or via email photos. For larger projects, we strongly recommend an in-home assessment for an accurate quote.
What areas do you service?
We serve 40+ neighborhoods in the greater metro area. If you're unsure whether we cover your area, call our main line or submit the booking form with your address — we'll confirm coverage within the hour.
