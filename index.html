FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? We have answers. Browse our most common questions or contact us directly.

How quickly can you respond to electrical emergencies?
Our emergency response guarantee is 60 minutes or less within our service area, 24/7/365 — including holidays. For life-threatening electrical emergencies involving fire or electrocution risk, always call 911 first. Our emergency line is 1-800-555-0199.
Are your electricians licensed and insured?
Yes. Every ElectraPro technician is a licensed master or journeyman electrician with current state certification. We carry $5M in general liability insurance and full workers' compensation coverage. All technicians are background-checked and drug-tested before hiring.
Do you provide free quotes and estimates?
Yes, we provide free in-home estimates for all projects. Our technician will assess your electrical needs and provide a detailed, itemized quote with upfront flat-rate pricing. There are no hidden fees or surprises — you approve the price before any work begins.
What warranty do you offer on your work?
All ElectraPro workmanship carries a 5-year warranty. If any issue arises from our electrical work within 5 years, we'll return and fix it at no charge. Parts and materials also include manufacturer warranties, typically ranging from 1–10 years depending on the component.
Can you handle both small repairs and large projects?
Absolutely. We handle everything from replacing a single outlet to complete home rewiring and new construction electrical. Our team includes specialists in smart home automation, solar integration, EV charging stations, and whole-home surge protection.
