Pricing FAQs

How are your prices determined?
Our prices are flat-rate, based on the specific job type, complexity, materials required, and local permit fees. We assess your home and provide a detailed itemized quote before beginning any work. You always know the total cost before we start.
Do you charge for travel or fuel?
No. There are no separate travel charges within our service area. The quoted price is the total price, including everything. Emergency call-outs have a standard call-out fee that covers emergency dispatch and travel.
Are permits included in the price?
Permit costs are included in our quoted price for jobs that require permits (such as panel upgrades and whole-home rewiring). We handle all permit applications and final inspections on your behalf.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards, check, and bank transfer. We also offer 0% financing for qualifying projects over $1,000 through our partnership with GreenSky Finance. Ask about our financing options when you receive your quote.
